Exporting Scoutbook & my.Scouting Data

Step-by-step export workflow to pull registered youth, parent rosters, and advancement records into the Pack 361 portal without den mismatches.

folder_zip
1
Authenticate as Key 3 or Unit Admin Log into my.Scouting.org or scoutbook.scouting.org with your official BSA Unit Administrator credentials.
2
Access Unit Roster in Internet Advancement 2.0 From the left drawer, navigate to Pack 361 > Roster or open Internet Advancement 2.0 to review active registrations.
3
Generate Comprehensive Roster CSV Click Export > Export Roster (.CSV). Confirm inclusion of BSA Member IDs, Cub legal names, parent primary email, and Den assignment numbers.
4
Export Purchasing & Needs-Approval Reports In Scoutbook Reports, select Needs Purchasing Report and Needs Approval to download pending belt loops and rank pins.
5
Sync to Pack Portal Drop the exported CSV file into the Webmaster portal dropzone below. Roster IDs, den feeds, and scout advancement rings update instantly.
lightbulb
Leader Cutoff Reminder

Export right after the monthly Pack Committee cutoff date (last Sunday of each month). This guarantees our Council Scout Shop order is submitted at least 10 days before Blue & Gold or Court of Honor celebrations.

Core Operational Playbooks

4 Modules
vpn_key
Parent Whitelist & Magic Links Access control & Youth Privacy
expand_more

To prevent unsolicited access to Pack 361 schedules and youth rosters, all parent logins use passwordless token links sent to the email address registered on their official BSA charter application.

Handling Transfer or New Scouts:
  • Check Council registrar confirmation in my.Scouting before provisioning app access.
  • Manually add parent email to the Pack 361 Firestore Whitelist under /authorized_parents.
  • New scouts will appear on their assigned den wall once the Scoutbook Member ID matches.
shield_person
Youth Protection (YPT) 2-Deep Photo SOP Media vault moderation & opt-outs
expand_more

Pack 361 strictly enforces BSA Youth Protection guidelines across the community Media Vault. Photos uploaded by den parents enter a staging queue requiring approval before appearing in public pack recaps.

visibility_off Photo Opt-Outs 3 scouts in Den 2 & Den 5 have signed parental opt-out waivers. Check the roster badge flag before publishing campout gallery highlights.
group Two-Leader Review Vault uploads require one registered leader stamp before sync. Never crop or tag scout full surnames on exterior sharing links.
offline_pin
Offline Cache & Emergency Alerts Field operations in zero-cell zones
expand_more

Some campsites (e.g., Broad Creek, western Maryland state parks) have weak or no cell signal. Den leaders must sync rosters to their local device cache before departing the church staging grounds.

cell_tower Emergency SMS Broadcast Trigger
In cases of sudden burn bans, severe storm evacuation, or campsite relocation, admins can broadcast an uncompressed SMS alert directly to all verified parent mobile numbers via Twilio failover.
