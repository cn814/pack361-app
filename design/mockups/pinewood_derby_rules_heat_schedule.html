Official BSA Car Specs

Pack 361 Inspection Standards

scale
Maximum Weight: 5.00 oz (141.7 g)

Measured strictly on the official digital scale at check-in. No loose weights permitted. Weights must be glued or securely recessed into car body.

aspect_ratio
Overall Dimensions

• Max Length: 7.00 inches
• Max Width: 2.75 inches (including wheels)
• Max Height: 3.00 inches (must clear electronic finish gate)

height
Track & Center Rail Clearance

Minimum bottom clearance of 3/8 in (0.375 in) between underside and track bed. Minimum 1 3/4 in inside wheel clearance across guide strip.

toll
Wheels, Axles & Lubrication

Official BSA kit wheels and axles only. Light seam removal & graphite burnishing allowed. No wheel beveling, lathe narrowing, ball bearings, or bushings. Dry graphite powder only — liquid oils (Krytox, WD40) strictly disqualified.

warning

Once a car passes the official weigh-in and dimension box test, it is impounded in the staging corral. No modifications or re-lubrication are allowed unless granted a pit repair by the Race Master.
