Broad Creek Camp Rules

1. Youth Protection: 2-deep adult leadership in effect 100% of the time. Adults must never be alone in a tent with youth other than their own child.

2. Buddy System: Scouts must maintain a registered scout buddy at all times, including trips to the latrine or water spigot.

3. Knives & Whittling Chip: Only scouts who have earned their Whittling Chip may carry pocketknives. No sheath knives allowed.

4. Quiet Hours: 9:30 PM to 6:30 AM. Generators strictly prohibited in tent areas.

5. Wildlife: Raccoons & deer frequent Pack Campsite. Keep all food in hard-sided vehicles overnight.
